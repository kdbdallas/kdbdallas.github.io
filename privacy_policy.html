Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Port21 ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, processes, and shares your information when you use our applications distributed across mobile, desktop, and television platforms (including iOS, macOS, tvOS, and Android), as well as any associated web portals or billing architectures.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you interact with inside our applications, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Data: If you choose to create an account to synchronize features or subscriptions across different platforms, we collect your email address and an encrypted hash of your password.
  • Transaction and Billing Data: For premium purchases and recurring subscriptions managed outside the native app stores, we collect basic transaction histories, email receipts, and billing references. Raw credit card data is securely handled entirely by our third-party processor and never stored on our systems.
  • Device and Usage Telemetry: To guarantee app stability, optimize performance, and resolve errors, we may process anonymous diagnostic logs, hardware model identifiers, operating system versions, and unique installation strings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to fulfill our operational agreements with you and under legitimate business interests, including to:

  • Authenticate your profile and maintain subscription sync across iOS, Mac, and Android platforms.
  • Validate and process transactions via external billing channels.
  • Deliver critical transactional and support emails directly related to your active accounts.
  • Diagnose performance bottlenecks, monitor software stability, and issue security updates.

3. Third-Party Service Providers and Processors

We leverage third-party partners to handle specialized technical and financial infrastructure. These entities are legally bound to protect your data and only utilize it to perform services on our behalf:

  • Stripe: Out-of-app-store commercial billing, invoicing, and subscription renewals are managed by Stripe. Your interactions are governed securely under the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Native App Store Processors: In-app purchases, microtransactions, and native storefront premium updates are routed directly through Apple Media Services or Google Play Billing infrastructure depending on your device ecosystem.

4. Data Retention and Safeguards

Account data and transaction records are preserved only as long as your application profile remains active, or as long as required to comply with strict statutory tax, legal, and financial reporting mandates. We employ modern physical, administrative, and industry-standard electronic token encapsulation protocols to shield data against unauthorized access, leaks, or exploitation.

5. Your Global Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your rights to retain control over your personal information. Depending on your home jurisdiction (such as the European Union under GDPR or the State of California under CCPA/CPRA), you possess the statutory right to:

  • Access: Request clear confirmation of whether your data is processed and obtain a portable copy of records.
  • Correction: Modify inaccurate profile info directly via your account settings.
  • Erasure: Request permanent removal of your account, email records, and custom settings.
  • Opt-Out: Decline telemetry or automated diagnostics processing where applicable.

To exercise any of these privileges, please reach out directly via the contact methods listed below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not intentionally request, gather, or compile personal data from individuals below the age of 13. If you suspect a minor has provisioned an account without parent or guardian consent, contact us immediately so we can execute swift deletion procedures.

7. Policy Amendments

We reserves the right to modify this statement periodically. Amendments will take effect immediately upon deployment to our live web site. We will alert you to major shifts by revising the "Last updated" metric at the top of this documentation.
