KDB Dashboard

KDB Dashboard turns your Apple TV into a dashboard for your home. A month calendar showing your Google and iCloud events fills the left side of the screen. On the right, a slideshow plays photos from an album you choose, above the weather for up to two zip codes. This page covers how to set up and use the App and how to reach us if something isn't working as expected. KDB Dashboard requires tvOS 17 or later.

Getting Started

The first time you open KDB Dashboard, it walks you through three steps:

  1. Photo Album — Choose Grant Access to let KDB Dashboard read your photo library, pick the album you want to see, and choose Next.
  2. Weather — Enter a 5-digit zip code for Location 1 and, if you like, a second one for Location 2, then choose Next. KDB Dashboard looks up each zip code before moving on.
  3. Calendars — Sign in with Google, iCloud, or both, as described below, then choose Next. At least one calendar is required.

You can change any of these later in Settings.

Connecting Your Calendars

  • Google Calendar — Choose Sign In with Google during setup, or Connect next to Google Calendar in Settings. KDB Dashboard shows a web address and a code. On your phone or computer, go to that address, enter the code, and sign in to your Google account. Your Apple TV connects on its own once you allow access, and you never type your Google password on the TV. KDB Dashboard shows the events in your primary Google Calendar and can only view them; it can't change them.
  • iCloud Calendar — iCloud calendars need an app-specific password instead of your Apple ID password. On your phone or computer, go to appleid.apple.com, sign in, open Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords, and create a password named "KDB Dashboard." Then, on your Apple TV, choose Sign In with iCloud during setup, or Connect next to iCloud Calendar in Settings. Enter your Apple ID email address and the app-specific password, and choose Sign In. You can enter the password with or without its dashes. KDB Dashboard shows the events in all of the calendars in your iCloud account.
  • Disconnecting — Open Settings and choose Disconnect next to Google Calendar or iCloud Calendar. This removes that account's sign-in from your Apple TV.

How to Use KDB Dashboard

  • Calendar — The left side of the screen shows the current month, with today highlighted in blue. Each day lists up to four events and their times, and shows how many more it has, such as "+2 more." An event that spans several days appears on each of them, with its start time on the first day and its end time on the last. Use the arrow buttons at the top to move between months, and choose Today to return to the current month and see today's events.
  • Event Details — Select a day to list all of its events, then select an event to see its time, location and a map of it, your RSVP status, meeting link, organizer, attendees and their responses, and notes. Choose Done or Close, or press the Back button (Menu on older remotes), to close them.
  • Upcoming Events — When an event today starts in the next 30 minutes, a banner above the calendar counts down to it, such as "Upcoming: Soccer Practice in 12 minutes." Once the event starts, the banner shows "Now" for three minutes, then goes away. All-day events don't show a banner.
  • Refresh — Your calendars refresh automatically every 5 minutes. Choose the refresh button at the top of the calendar to check right away.
  • Photos — The upper right shows a photo from your album, and changes to a new one every 3 minutes unless you change it in Settings. Photos appear in random order, and every photo in the album is shown before any of them repeats.
  • Weather — The lower right shows the current temperature and conditions, today's high and low, how warm it feels, and the humidity, along with the day and time. The weather updates every 30 minutes. If you entered two zip codes, the weather switches between them every 15 minutes; to switch right away, highlight the weather and swipe up or down.
  • Weather Details — Select the weather to see a 5-day forecast, along with the UV index, wind and gusts, pressure, visibility, dew point, cloud cover, and the chance and amount of rain or snow. Choose Done, or press the Back button (Menu on older remotes), to close it.
  • Settings — Choose the gear button at the top of the calendar to connect or disconnect your calendars, choose a different photo album, set how often the photo changes (every 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes), and change your weather zip codes.
  • Always On — While KDB Dashboard is open, it keeps your Apple TV awake, so the screen saver doesn't start.

What the Colors Mean

  • Orange — An event from Google Calendar.
  • Blue — An event from iCloud Calendar.
  • Dimmed — An event earlier today that has already ended.

In an event's details, your RSVP status and each attendee's response are shown as Accepted in green, Maybe in yellow, Declined in red, or No Response in gray.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the information come from?
Your events come directly from Google Calendar and iCloud, your photos come from your Apple TV's photo library, and the weather comes from Apple Weather. KDB Dashboard doesn't have its own servers; everything goes straight between your Apple TV and Apple or Google.

Is KDB Dashboard made by Apple or Google?
No. KDB Dashboard is an independent App and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc. or Google LLC.

Which calendars does KDB Dashboard show?
For Google, it shows your primary calendar, the one named after your Google account. Other Google calendars you've created or subscribed to aren't shown. For iCloud, it shows all of the calendars in your iCloud account.

Why don't I see events in later months?
KDB Dashboard loads events from the week before the current month through about the middle of next month. Months further ahead appear empty until they get closer.

I changed an event on my phone. When will it show up?
Within 5 minutes, when the calendar next refreshes. To see it right away, choose the refresh button at the top of the calendar.

I see "Authentication failed. Check your Apple ID and app-specific password." What should I do?
Make sure you entered the email address for your Apple ID and an app-specific password, not your regular Apple ID password. App-specific passwords are only available when your Apple ID uses two-factor authentication. Changing your Apple ID password, or revoking the app-specific password at appleid.apple.com, also stops it from working. In that case, create a new app-specific password, choose Disconnect next to iCloud Calendar in Settings, and connect again.

Google sign-in says "Authorization timed out" or "Authorization expired."
The sign-in code only works for a limited time. Choose Sign In with Google, or Connect in Settings, to get a new code, and enter it on your phone or computer right away. "Access denied by user" means access wasn't allowed on the other device; try again and choose Allow.

Why is KDB Dashboard asking me to connect my calendar again?
This happens when the App's access to your calendar ends, for example if you removed KDB Dashboard from your Google Account permissions. Connect the calendar again to continue.

My photo album isn't in the list. Why?
KDB Dashboard lists the albums you've created and built-in albums like Favorites, as long as they contain at least one photo. Shared albums aren't listed, and videos are skipped. If none of your albums appear, make sure iCloud Photos is turned on in your Apple TV's Settings, under Users and Accounts.

The photo area says the album wasn't found.
The album was probably renamed or deleted. Open Settings, choose Change next to your photo album, and pick it again or choose another one.

The photo area says "Photo Access Denied."
Open your Apple TV's Settings app and allow KDB Dashboard to access your photos in its privacy settings. Then quit and reopen KDB Dashboard: double-press the TV button on your remote, then swipe up on KDB Dashboard to close it.

Why isn't my zip code accepted?
KDB Dashboard uses 5-digit US zip codes, and looks each one up before saving it. Check that the zip code is correct and try again. If a zip code you enter in Settings can't be found, it isn't saved and the previous one stays.

How do I remove my second weather location?
Open Settings, choose Change under Weather Location, select Location 2, delete the zip code, choose OK, then choose Save.

Can the weather be shown in Celsius?
Not at this time. Temperatures are shown in Fahrenheit and wind speeds in miles per hour.

The weather says "Unable to fetch weather."
This means KDB Dashboard couldn't reach Apple Weather. Check your Apple TV's internet connection. The App tries again every 30 minutes.

Does KDB Dashboard require an internet connection?
Yes. KDB Dashboard needs an internet connection to get your events, the weather, and any photos stored in iCloud Photos.

How do I start over?
Open Settings and choose Disconnect next to each connected calendar, then delete KDB Dashboard and install it again. The setup steps run again the next time you open it.

Does KDB Dashboard collect any of my data?
No. KDB Dashboard doesn't require an account and doesn't collect, track, or share any personal information. Your settings and calendar sign-ins are only stored on your Apple TV. See our Privacy Policy for details.
