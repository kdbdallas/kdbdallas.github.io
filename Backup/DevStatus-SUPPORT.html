Dev Status

Dev Status shows the current status of developer services from Apple, GitHub, DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, Ably, Bitbucket, Buildkite, CircleCI, Codecov, Confluence, Expo, GitLab, HashiCorp, Jira, Snyk, Travis CI, Trello, RubyGems, crates.io, AWS, and Fly.io, such as App Store Connect, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Workers, Docker Hub, and Bitbucket Pipelines, in the App and in Home Screen widgets, and can notify you when a service's status changes. This page covers how to use the App and how to reach us if something isn't working as expected.

How to Use Dev Status

  • Service List — Open the App to see every provider, listed alphabetically. Tap a provider to show its services, also listed alphabetically, and their current status, and tap it again to hide them. Each provider shows its most serious status and how many of its services have issues or maintenance, even while collapsed. When the provider has posted a message about an issue or maintenance, it appears below the service name. A long message shows its first four lines; tap More to read the rest, and Less to shorten it again. For DigitalOcean, Ably, and HashiCorp's Consul Dedicated and Vault Dedicated, a product shows an issue if any of its regions has one, and the message starts with the affected region, such as "NYC3." Snyk lists each of its data regions, such as "Snyk US-01 (app.snyk.io)," as one service, and its messages start with the affected products, such as "Snyk Container." AWS lists each of its regions as one service, such as "AWS us-east-1 (N. Virginia)," plus "AWS Global" for products like CloudFront, and its messages start with the affected AWS products. Postman services available in both its US and Europe regions are listed once per region, such as "Postman Login (US)" and "Postman Login (Europe)." OpenAI, Docker, HashiCorp, and Fly.io services show their status, such as "Partial Outage," without a message. Use the search field to find a service by name.
  • Refresh — Pull down on the list or tap the refresh button to get the latest status. "Last Updated" at the top of the list shows how long ago the status was checked.
  • Automatic Updates — While the App is open, Dev Status automatically checks for new status information once the current data is older than the refresh interval, 15 minutes unless you change it in Settings.
  • Settings — Tap the gear button to choose your subscribed services and how often Dev Status refreshes: every 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.
  • Home Screen Widgets — Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap "Edit," then "Add Widget," and search for Dev Status. Widgets come in small, medium, and large sizes, and each size shows as many of your subscribed services as fit, with any that have issues first. When they don't all fit, each service gets a single line so more fit. Tap a service in a medium or large widget to open it in the App.
  • Choose Your Services — Open Settings > Subscribed Services in the App and tap a service to subscribe to it. Every Dev Status widget shows the services you subscribe to.
  • Notifications — Allow notifications when prompted and Dev Status will alert you when a service you subscribe to reports an issue, has maintenance scheduled or in progress, or returns to normal. Choose your services in Settings > Subscribed Services.

What the Colors Mean

  • Green — Operational. No current issues.
  • Yellow — Upcoming Maintenance. The provider has scheduled maintenance; the start and end times are shown on their own line, above the message.
  • Orange — Maintenance. Maintenance is currently in progress.
  • Red — Issue. The service is experiencing an issue or outage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the status information come from?
Dev Status reads the same information Apple publishes on its Developer System Status page, GitHub publishes on githubstatus.com, DigitalOcean publishes on status.digitalocean.com, OpenAI publishes on status.openai.com, Cloudflare publishes on cloudflarestatus.com, Docker publishes on dockerstatus.com, npm publishes on status.npmjs.org, Postman publishes on status.postman.com, Ably publishes on status.ably.com, Bitbucket publishes on bitbucket.status.atlassian.com, Buildkite publishes on buildkitestatus.com, CircleCI publishes on status.circleci.com, Codecov publishes on status.codecov.com, Confluence publishes on confluence.status.atlassian.com, Expo publishes on status.expo.dev, GitLab publishes on status.gitlab.com, HashiCorp publishes on status.hashicorp.com, Jira publishes on jira-software.status.atlassian.com, Snyk publishes on status.snyk.io, Travis CI publishes on traviscistatus.com, Trello publishes on trello.status.atlassian.com, RubyGems publishes on status.rubygems.org, crates.io publishes on status.crates.io, AWS publishes on its Health Dashboard, and Fly.io publishes on status.flyio.net. Cloudflare's individual data centers aren't shown, only its products, and neither are the Fastly locations RubyGems lists. If a provider hasn't posted an issue or maintenance event, Dev Status won't show one.

Is Dev Status made by any of the companies whose status it shows?
No. Dev Status is an independent App and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc., GitHub, Inc., DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, Ably, Atlassian, Buildkite, CircleCI, Harness, Expo, GitLab, HashiCorp, Snyk, Travis CI, RubyGems, the Rust Foundation, Amazon Web Services, or Fly.io.

How often does the status update?
In the App, status is refreshed when it's older than the refresh interval, which is 15 minutes unless you change it in Settings, and you can pull down to refresh at any time. Widgets update about as often as the refresh interval, but iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so updates may sometimes take longer.

Why didn't I get a notification?
First, make sure you're subscribed to the service: Dev Status only sends alerts for the services you choose in the App's Settings > Subscribed Services. Then make sure notifications are turned on in Settings > Notifications > Dev Status. Dev Status checks for changes when the App refreshes or when one of its widgets updates, so adding a Dev Status widget to your Home Screen lets it check for changes even when the App isn't open.

My widget says "No subscribed services." How do I fix it?
Tap the widget, or open the App and go to Settings > Subscribed Services, and subscribe to one or more services. Use the search field to find a service by name, such as "TestFlight," "GitHub Actions," or "Droplets."

Why does Dev Status show something different from a provider's status page?
Dev Status may be up to one refresh interval (15 minutes by default) behind their status pages. Open the App and pull down to refresh to get the latest status.

I see "Failed to fetch status." What should I do?
This means Dev Status couldn't reach any of the status pages. If only some of them are unreachable, Dev Status keeps showing those providers' most recent status and doesn't show this message. Check your internet connection and try refreshing again. If Dev Status has checked before, it will keep showing the most recent status it received.

Does Dev Status require an internet connection?
Yes. Dev Status needs an internet connection to get the latest status from each provider. Without one, it shows the most recent status it saved.

Does Dev Status collect any of my data?
No. Dev Status doesn't require an account and doesn't collect, track, or share any personal information. Status information is only stored on your device so the App and widgets can share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
