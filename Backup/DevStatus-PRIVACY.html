Privacy Policy

Dev Status · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Dev Status ("the App") shows the current status of developer services from Apple, GitHub, DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, Ably, Bitbucket, Buildkite, CircleCI, Codecov, Confluence, Expo, GitLab, HashiCorp, Jira, Snyk, Travis CI, Trello, RubyGems, crates.io, AWS, and Fly.io. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information We Do Not Collect

Dev Status does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require an account, login, or sign-up
  • Does not send any information about you or your device to us
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies
  • Does not use any third-party software development kits (SDKs) or services
  • Does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos, or microphone
  • Does not use cookies

How the App Works

To show service status, the App downloads Apple's public Developer System Status information directly from apple.com, GitHub's public status information directly from githubstatus.com, DigitalOcean's public status information directly from status.digitalocean.com, OpenAI's public status information directly from status.openai.com, Cloudflare's public status information directly from www.cloudflarestatus.com, Docker's public status information directly from www.dockerstatus.com, npm's public status information directly from status.npmjs.org, Postman's public status information directly from status.postman.com, Ably's public status information directly from status.ably.com, Bitbucket's public status information directly from bitbucket.status.atlassian.com, Buildkite's public status information directly from www.buildkitestatus.com, CircleCI's public status information directly from status.circleci.com, Codecov's public status information directly from status.codecov.com, Confluence's public status information directly from confluence.status.atlassian.com, Expo's public status information directly from status.expo.dev, GitLab's public status information directly from status.io, which hosts GitLab's status page, HashiCorp's public status information directly from status.hashicorp.com, Jira's public status information directly from jira-software.status.atlassian.com, Snyk's public status information directly from status.snyk.io, Travis CI's public status information directly from www.traviscistatus.com, Trello's public status information directly from trello.status.atlassian.com, RubyGems' public status information directly from status.rubygems.org, crates.io's public status information directly from status.crates.io, AWS's public status information directly from health.aws.amazon.com, and Fly.io's public status information directly from status.flyio.net. These are standard web requests that do not include any personal information, and we never see them. As with any website you visit, Apple, GitHub, DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, Ably, Atlassian (which runs Bitbucket, Confluence, Jira, and Trello), Buildkite, CircleCI, Harness (which runs Codecov), Expo, Status.io (which hosts GitLab's status page), HashiCorp, Snyk, Travis CI, RubyGems, the Rust Foundation (which runs crates.io), Amazon Web Services, and Fly.io receive the network information that comes with those requests, such as your IP address, and handle it under Apple's Privacy Policy, the GitHub General Privacy Statement, DigitalOcean's Privacy Policy, OpenAI's Privacy Policy, Cloudflare's Privacy Policy, Docker's Privacy Policy, npm's Privacy Policy, Postman's Privacy Policy, Ably's Privacy Policy, Atlassian's Privacy Policy, Buildkite's Privacy Policy, CircleCI's Privacy Policy, Harness's Privacy Policy, Expo's Privacy Policy, Status.io's Privacy Policy, HashiCorp's Privacy Policy, Snyk's Privacy Policy, Travis CI's Privacy Policy, RubyGems' Privacy Policy, the Rust Foundation's Privacy Policy, the AWS Privacy Notice, and Fly.io's Privacy Policy.

The most recent status information is saved on your device so the App and its Home Screen widgets can display it and detect when a service's status changes. This saved data contains only these providers' service status information, stays on your device, and is removed when you delete the App.

Notifications

The App asks for permission to send notifications so it can alert you when a service's status changes. These notifications are created on your device; the App does not use push notification servers and does not register your device with us or anyone else. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Notifications > Dev Status.

Children's Privacy

Because Dev Status does not collect any information from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age.

Third-Party Services

Dev Status does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other services. The only outside services the App contacts are Apple's Developer System Status page and the GitHub, DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, Ably, Bitbucket, Buildkite, CircleCI, Codecov, Confluence, Expo, GitLab, HashiCorp, Jira, Snyk, Travis CI, Trello, RubyGems, crates.io, AWS, and Fly.io status pages, as described above.
