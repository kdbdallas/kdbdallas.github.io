Dev Status

Dev Status shows the current status of developer services from Apple, GitHub, DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, and Ably, such as App Store Connect, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Workers, and Docker Hub, in the App and in Home Screen widgets, and can notify you when a service's status changes. This page covers how to use the App and how to reach us if something isn't working as expected.

How to Use Dev Status

  • Service List — Open the App to see every provider, listed alphabetically. Tap a provider to show its services, also listed alphabetically, and their current status, and tap it again to hide them. Each provider shows its most serious status and how many of its services have issues or maintenance, even while collapsed. When the provider has posted a message about an issue or maintenance, it appears below the service name. For DigitalOcean and Ably, a product shows an issue if any of its regions has one, and the message starts with the affected region, such as "NYC3." Postman services available in both its US and Europe regions are listed once per region, such as "Postman Login (US)" and "Postman Login (Europe)." OpenAI and Docker services show their status, such as "Partial Outage," without a message. Use the search field to find a service by name.
  • Refresh — Pull down on the list or tap the refresh button to get the latest status. "Last Updated" at the top of the list shows how long ago the status was checked.
  • Automatic Updates — While the App is open, Dev Status automatically checks for new status information once the current data is more than 15 minutes old.
  • Home Screen Widgets — Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap "Edit," then "Add Widget," and search for Dev Status. Widgets come in small (one service), medium (up to three services), and large (up to five services) sizes.
  • Choose Widget Services — Touch and hold a Dev Status widget, tap "Edit Widget," and then tap "Services to Monitor" to choose which services that widget shows.
  • Notifications — Allow notifications when prompted and Dev Status will alert you when a service reports an issue, has maintenance scheduled or in progress, or returns to normal.

What the Colors Mean

  • Green — Operational. No current issues.
  • Yellow — Upcoming Maintenance. The provider has scheduled maintenance; the start and end times are shown with the message.
  • Orange — Maintenance. Maintenance is currently in progress.
  • Red — Issue. The service is experiencing an issue or outage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the status information come from?
Dev Status reads the same information Apple publishes on its Developer System Status page, GitHub publishes on githubstatus.com, DigitalOcean publishes on status.digitalocean.com, OpenAI publishes on status.openai.com, Cloudflare publishes on cloudflarestatus.com, Docker publishes on dockerstatus.com, npm publishes on status.npmjs.org, Postman publishes on status.postman.com, and Ably publishes on status.ably.com. Cloudflare's individual data centers aren't shown, only its products. If a provider hasn't posted an issue or maintenance event, Dev Status won't show one.

Is Dev Status made by any of the companies whose status it shows?
No. Dev Status is an independent App and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc., GitHub, Inc., DigitalOcean, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Docker, npm, Postman, or Ably.

How often does the status update?
In the App, status is refreshed when it's more than 15 minutes old, and you can pull down to refresh at any time. Widgets update about every 15 minutes, but iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so updates may sometimes take longer.

Why didn't I get a notification?
First, make sure notifications are turned on in Settings > Notifications > Dev Status. Dev Status checks for changes when the App refreshes or when one of its widgets updates, so adding a Dev Status widget to your Home Screen lets it check for changes even when the App isn't open.

My widget says "No services selected." How do I fix it?
Touch and hold the widget, tap "Edit Widget," and choose one or more services under "Services to Monitor." Use the search field at the top of the list to find a service by name, such as "TestFlight," "GitHub Actions," or "Droplets."

Why does Dev Status show something different from a provider's status page?
Dev Status may be up to 15 minutes behind their status pages. Open the App and pull down to refresh to get the latest status.

I see "Failed to fetch status." What should I do?
This means Dev Status couldn't reach any of the status pages. If only some of them are unreachable, Dev Status keeps showing those providers' most recent status and doesn't show this message. Check your internet connection and try refreshing again. If Dev Status has checked before, it will keep showing the most recent status it received.

Does Dev Status require an internet connection?
Yes. Dev Status needs an internet connection to get the latest status from each provider. Without one, it shows the most recent status it saved.

Does Dev Status collect any of my data?
No. Dev Status doesn't require an account and doesn't collect, track, or share any personal information. Status information is only stored on your device so the App and widgets can share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
