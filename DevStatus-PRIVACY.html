Privacy Policy

Dev Status · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Dev Status ("the App") shows the current status of Apple's developer services. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information We Do Not Collect

Dev Status does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require an account, login, or sign-up
  • Does not send any information about you or your device to us
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies
  • Does not use any third-party software development kits (SDKs) or services
  • Does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos, or microphone
  • Does not use cookies

How the App Works

To show service status, the App downloads Apple's public Developer System Status information directly from apple.com. This is a standard web request that does not include any personal information, and we never see it. As with any website you visit, Apple receives the network information that comes with that request, such as your IP address, and handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

The most recent status information is saved on your device so the App and its Home Screen widgets can display it and detect when a service's status changes. This saved data contains only Apple's service status information, stays on your device, and is removed when you delete the App.

Notifications

The App asks for permission to send notifications so it can alert you when a service's status changes. These notifications are created on your device; the App does not use push notification servers and does not register your device with us or anyone else. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Notifications > Dev Status.

Children's Privacy

Because Dev Status does not collect any information from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age.

Third-Party Services

Dev Status does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other services. The only outside service the App contacts is Apple's Developer System Status page, as described above.
